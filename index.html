FROM SEARCH TO A SHORTLIST

Find relevant records. Know what is missing.

1. Search the records

Enter an operator, certificate designator, or aircraft model. Browse the FAA-derived directory and inspect the recorded fleet.

2. Compare on evidence

Use aircraft type and the completeness of available record details as research signals. More complete data can make a record easier to evaluate, not better or safer.

3. Ask the provider

Confirm service area, the aircraft, availability, all-in pricing, and operating details directly with a qualified provider.

What “recommended” can mean here: relevance to your entered operator or aircraft terms and completeness of public record data—not an automated quality or safety ranking. The current directory searches records; it does not yet generate personalized trip recommendations.

FAA records do not include reliable service areas, live inventory, availability, verified trip pricing, or booking/quote capability. A listing is not an endorsement or a suitability guarantee.
