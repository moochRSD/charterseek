THE NUMBER THAT MATTERS

A headline hourly rate is not a trip price.

For short trips, daily minimums and aircraft positioning often matter more than the advertised hourly rate. Ask every provider where the aircraft is based, how many hours will be billed, and whether taxes and handling are included.

QUOTE CHECKLIST

Four questions before you compare offers

  1. Where is the aircraft based?Ask whether positioning time is billed and at what rate.
  2. What is the daily minimum?Confirm the number of billable hours, not only flight time.
  3. Is fuel fixed or floating?Clarify whether a surcharge could be added later.
  4. What is the all-in amount?Request every fee, tax, handling charge and overnight cost in writing.
